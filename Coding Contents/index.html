Inknut Antiqua

“Inknut Antiqua is an Antiqua typeface for literature and long-form text. Approaching the idea of web-publishing as a
modern day private press, it is designed to evoke Venetian incunabula and humanist manuscripts, but with the quirks and
idiosyncrasies of the kinds of typefaces you find in this artisanal tradition. It comes with a complement of typographical
sorts and OpenType features for the purpose. The proportions of Inknut Antiqua make it well suited for low-resolution
screens. The Inknut (Terminalia Chebula), called "harad" in Hindi, is a nut-like fruit that can be used for ink making and is
purported to cure blindness. The tree it grows from is native to the Indian sub-continent and south-east Asia. The Inknut
Antiqua project is led by Claus Eggers Sørensen, a type designer based in Amsterdam.”

Natural skincare uses plant-based ingredients that are
suitable for sensitive skin. Skincare created with synthetic
materials is usually harsher on the skin’s barrier and easily
causes redness and trigger underlying skin issues. Natural
ingredients often contain vitamins that the skin needs to stay
nourished and hydrated, such as aloe vera and shea butter.
However, it is important to recognize that many synthetic
skincare products try to incorporate natural ingredients to
promote themselves as “natural,” but they could still include
many artificial dyes and fragrances.

Focusing on physical and mental well-being is so important
because it increases our longevity and fosters a happier,
healthier life. Regularly focusing on physical activity can help
you sleep better and feel better during the day, and it even has
the huge, added benefit of reducing the risk of some cancers,
heart disease, and diabetes. The sooner we start taking care
of our well-being, the better off we will be in our old age.

Botanical gardens feature a wide range of living plants in
meticulously curated outdoor and indoor spaces.  Some of the
most popular botanical gardens in the Illinois region include
The Morton Arboretum, Anderson Japanese Gardens, and the
Chicago Botanic Garden.  Strolling through one of these nature
centers is a great way to educate yourself about plant
conservation and appreciate nature’s beauty.
